## 🌍 Our Commitment

HCC Analytics BD is intended to be a welcoming learning and portfolio environment for people working with HR, payroll, compliance, business intelligence and data analytics.

Maintainers, contributors and participants are expected to help create a space that is:

- respectful across differences in experience, identity, background and viewpoint;
- focused on evidence, data quality and constructive problem-solving;
- safe for questions, feedback and professional disagreement;
- responsible in the handling of HR, payroll and compliance information;
- free from harassment, intimidation and retaliation.

---

## ✅ Expected Behaviour

| Principle | Expected practice |
|---|---|
| **Respect** | Address people and their work professionally, even when disagreeing. |
| **Constructive feedback** | Explain the issue, its impact and a practical improvement path. |
| **Accuracy** | Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, synthetic examples and recommendations. |
| **Privacy** | Never expose personal, applicant, employee, payroll, medical, tax or banking information. |
| **Responsible analytics** | Avoid misleading visualisations, unsupported legal conclusions or discriminatory interpretations. |
| **Collaboration** | Keep pull requests focused, document decisions and respond to review comments in good faith. |
| **Accessibility** | Prefer clear language, readable formatting and interpretable charts or tables. |

Examples of positive participation include:

- asking clarifying questions before making assumptions;
- acknowledging and correcting mistakes;
- crediting contributors and sources appropriately;
- helping newcomers understand the repository structure;
- separating personal criticism from technical review;
- reporting security or privacy risks privately.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not permitted:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation or sustained disruption;
- personal attacks, insults, ridicule or hostile language;
- sexualised language, imagery or unwanted attention;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- impersonation, deceptive attribution or deliberate misinformation;
- retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith;
- introducing real confidential HR or organisational data into the repository;
- uploading credentials, tokens, identity records, payroll bank details or restricted documents;
- intentionally manipulating calculations, datasets or visuals to mislead users;
- using the project to profile, target or discriminate against real individuals or groups.

> [!CAUTION]
> Real employee records, CVs, medical information, national identifiers, salary sheets, tax data and bank details must never be committed to this public repository.

---

## 📊 Project-Specific Data Ethics

Because this repository models sensitive HR and payroll workflows, contributors must apply a higher standard of care.

### Required

- Use synthetic data by default.
- Clearly label demo assumptions and configurable formulas.
- Validate totals before presenting conclusions.
- Explain analytical limitations and potential bias.
- Use aggregated reporting where individual-level detail is unnecessary.
- Follow the repository's [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Not acceptable

- Presenting demo calculations as confirmed legal or payroll advice.
- Claiming discrimination, fraud or misconduct without sufficient evidence.
- Re-identifying anonymised or synthetic records.
- Adding hidden external connections, macros or credentials to binary files.

## 📨 Reporting a Concern

Do not post sensitive conduct, privacy or security details in a public issue.

Report concerns privately through one of the following routes:

1. Use GitHub **Private vulnerability reporting** from the repository's **Security** tab when the concern involves security or sensitive data.
2. Contact the repository maintainer through the contact options available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
3. Share only the minimum information necessary to establish a private communication channel.

A useful report includes:

- where the behaviour occurred;
- a concise description of what happened;
- relevant links, screenshots or timestamps;
- any immediate privacy or safety risk;
- the outcome requested, when appropriate.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information may be shared only with people necessary to assess and resolve the concern.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Maintainers may apply proportionate action based on severity, repetition, context and impact.

| Level | Example response |
|---|---|
| **1 — Guidance** | Private clarification, correction request or reminder of project standards. |
| **2 — Warning** | Formal warning and required corrective action. |
| **3 — Restriction** | Temporary limitation on participation, commenting or contribution. |
| **4 — Removal** | Rejection or reversal of harmful content and removal from active participation. |
| **5 — Ban or escalation** | Permanent ban and, where necessary, escalation to GitHub or relevant authorities. |

Maintainers may remove content immediately when it exposes sensitive data, credentials, security details or a credible safety risk.

---

## 🔒 No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern, supports a report or participates in a review in good faith is prohibited.

False or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a conduct violation. A report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically considered malicious.
